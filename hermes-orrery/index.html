About the data

Orbital elements are the standard J2000 Keplerian sets (semi-major axis, eccentricity, inclination, node, perihelion, mean longitude) — the same numbers used in classic ephemerides, propagated with a Kepler-equation solver each frame. Distances are true (1 AU = 10 units). Planet sizes are cinematic by default; True scale switches every body to its real ratio (bodies enlarged ×20 so they stay visible).

The Moon, the asteroid belt (≈1,600 bodies on independent Kepler orbits), Saturn's rings and the star field are all simulated. Spin rates use real sidereal periods — Venus and Uranus spin retrograde.

Rendered with three.js (r160) + bloom post-processing.
